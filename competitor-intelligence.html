Competitor Intelligence Engine

Competitor Analysis · E-commerce Market Intelligence

Every competitor's price, assortment, and promotion is a signal, but most brands just can't see it fast enough to act on it.

ContextPromptCloud / 42Signals
DomainE-commerce
StackPython · Elasticsearch · Kibana
StatusConfidential Client Work

Most brands can tell you their own catalog in exhaustive detail: pricing, stock, promotions, down to the SKU. Ask what a specific competitor did in the same categories this week, and the answer is usually a guess from a manual spot-check, done too rarely to catch anything before it costs margin or shelf share.

The gap isn't a lack of data; competitor pricing and assortment sit in plain sight on public storefronts. The gap is that nobody's watching continuously.

PythonElasticsearchKibanaMySQL

The Build

01 Problem

A business rarely has a clean, current list of which marketplace products are actually competing with its own. A manually maintained competitor list becomes outdated the moment it's written down.

02 Data

Product names, inferred brands, categories, selling and list prices, and marketplace metadata, collected across competing storefronts on a recurring schedule — the same signals a manual spot-check would use, just watched continuously instead of occasionally.

03 Approach

A monitoring pipeline tracks competitor pricing, assortment, and positioning across marketplaces, normalizing listings and mapping them to competitors before scoring how confident the system is in a given match or change.

04 Validation
A pricing dashboard nobody trusts is worse than no dashboard at all.

Most of the engineering effort didn't go into collecting prices; it went into deciding when not to trust one. A page that fails to load and gets read as "price dropped to ₹0" is worse than a missing data point, because it's confidently wrong. So the pipeline spends more logic distinguishing a real price change from a broken page, a temporary stockout, or a currency formatting glitch than it does on the scrape itself — rule-based comparison plus confidence scoring to separate real competitor moves from noise.

05 Result

This is confidential client work, so the exact detection logic and results stay private. What's shared here is the shape of the system: a recurring, automated comparison replacing a manual, occasional one.

06 Impact

Competitive visibility becomes a dashboard a brand checks, rather than a project someone has to remember to run. The lesson generalizes: in any monitoring system, the unglamorous work of not lying to your users about data you're unsure of matters more than the flashy part of collecting it.
