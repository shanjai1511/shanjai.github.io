About this blog.

Beyond the Obvious is a small collection of essays about the gap between what's easy to see and what's actually true. Not contrarianism for its own sake: just a habit of asking what the first explanation left out.

The topics wander: decisions, systems, work, the occasional observation with no home elsewhere. What ties them together is the same question, asked in different rooms: what would I notice here if I weren't already sure what I'd find?

New posts arrive whenever there's something worth the second look, not on a schedule.
