Enhanced Chemistry Lab LMS & CLECTS

An AI-guided virtual lab, built before "AI-powered" was the default pitch

A physical chemistry lab is only open for a few hours a week, but a student's confusion doesn't keep the same schedule. This project asks what a lab looks like when the help is always on.

TypeAcademic · Published
Access24/7 Virtual
StackPython · Langchain · BeautifulSoup
SourceView on GitHub ↗

This started as coursework, but the underlying question stuck with me: physical lab constraints (limited slots, limited supervision) leave students without guided help exactly when they're stuck on a procedure, usually outside lab hours. A textbook can't answer a follow-up question; a professor isn't available at 11 p.m. the night before an exam.

The approach combines web-scraped chemistry content with a Langchain-powered LLM and voice commands, so a student can ask a question mid-procedure instead of abandoning it. It also produced two published papers, ECLLMS and CLECTS, the latter extending the same idea into collaborative, instructor-student experiment annotation.

It's the project that taught me the most about combining retrieval with generation before "RAG" was the term everyone used for it, and that grounding still shows up in how I think about SARA's architecture today.

The Build

Challenge

Physical lab constraints left students without 24/7 guided help; complex procedures were difficult to follow independently outside limited lab hours.

Approach

Combined web-scraped chemistry content with a Langchain LLM and voice commands to create a fully interactive virtual lab that answers questions in context.
