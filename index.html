About Me

I work where messy data meets real-world systems.

RoleSoftware Engineer II
CompanyPromptCloud / 42Signals
LocationCoimbatore, India
LanguagesTamil · English

I work where messy data meets real-world systems. As a data scientist, my work sits across data engineering, analytics and e-commerce intelligence — building pipelines, processing systems and analytical workflows that turn raw marketplace and web data into something teams can actually use, including SARA, my current work in fashion and retail intelligence.

I tend to look beyond the immediate technical problem. When data fails, I want to understand why. When a pipeline works for five sites, I ask whether it will still work for a hundred. And when a dashboard contains thousands of metrics, I want to know which of them can actually change a decision — the kind of thinking laid out in more detail in how I think about systems.

Over the last few years, I have worked across data collection, processing, validation, infrastructure, analytics and intelligence systems. This has given me a perspective that sits somewhere between engineering and problem-solving: build reliable systems, understand the data flowing through them, and keep the final business decision in view. See what's changed because of that work for a few concrete examples.

I'm currently expanding that foundation through my M.Tech in Data Science at BITS Pilani, while continuing to work on data-driven systems in the e-commerce intelligence space.

The Person Who Knows How the Company Works Has More Leverage Than the Person Who Knows the Most

Understanding why a system was built a certain way is worth more than knowing the most code. Two pipelines that looked logically separate turned out to be split only because of an outdated client requirement; recognizing that led to roughly a 50% reduction in analytics cost. "The code tells you what. The history tells you why."
