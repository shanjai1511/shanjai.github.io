Engineering Decision Simulator

Scenario: fetch 50,000 product pages within a 4-hour maintenance window. Pick an approach.

What is happening?

Each approach has a realistic sustained request rate: slower, safer approaches make fewer requests per second but carry less risk of getting rate-limited or blocked, which resets the clock further than a purely slow-but-steady pace would. The completion time is real division (50,000 ÷ requests/sec), not a made-up estimate.

Under the Hood
Approach selection↓Completion time = 50,000 ÷ approach's request rate↓Compared against the 4-hour deadline

Inputs

  • Fixed workload: 50,000 pages
  • 4-hour deadline

Processing

  • Rate-based time-to-completion calculation

Output

  • Completion time
  • Pass / fail against the deadline
  • Risk notes

Technology

  • Vanilla JavaScript
