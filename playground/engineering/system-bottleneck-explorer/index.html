A simplified model: treats each stage as a straight-line throughput with no queuing, retries or parallelism, to isolate the bottleneck idea.

What is happening?

A pipeline can only move as fast as its slowest stage: the system throughput is the minimum of the six stage throughputs, not their average. Speeding up a stage that isn't the bottleneck changes nothing; the whole point of finding the bottleneck first is that it's the only place extra capacity actually helps.

Under the Hood
Six stage throughputs (items/min) ↓ System throughput = min(all six) ↓ Bottleneck stage highlighted, batch time estimated
