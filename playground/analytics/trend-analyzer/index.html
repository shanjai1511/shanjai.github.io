Trend Analyzer

Track a metric over time, filtered by date range.

Demonstration using a representative 30-day sample dataset.

What is happening?

The trend line is a linear regression of the metric against the day index within your selected date range — a positive slope means the metric is generally climbing over that window, even with day-to-day noise. Narrowing the range can flip the trend direction; that's expected, and part of why the range matters as much as the metric.

Under the Hood
Date-range selection↓Rows filtered to the range↓Linear regression of metric vs. day index

Inputs

  • 30 daily rows: orders, average order value

Processing

  • Date filtering
  • Least-squares trend line

Output

  • Line chart with trend overlay
  • Trend direction + summary stats

Technology

  • Vanilla JavaScript
  • Hand-built inline SVG chart
