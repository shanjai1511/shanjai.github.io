📊 Data Analytics

Distribution Explorer

Pick a column. See its shape, spread and outliers.

Demonstration using a representative 20-row sample dataset.

Summary Statistics

What is happening?

The histogram splits the column's range into 8 equal-width buckets and counts how many values fall in each: a quick read on whether the data is roughly symmetric, skewed, or clustered. The outlier count uses the IQR rule: any value more than 1.5× the interquartile range beyond the 25th or 75th percentile.

Under the Hood
Column selection ↓ 8-bucket histogram over the column's range ↓ Mean, median, stdev, quartiles, IQR outliers

Inputs

  • 5 numeric columns, 20 rows

Processing

  • Equal-width bucketing
  • IQR outlier rule

Output

  • Histogram
  • Summary statistics

Technology

  • Vanilla JavaScript
  • Hand-built histogram bars
