Price Violation Detector

Pick a brand. See which marketplace listings fall below its price floor.

Demonstration using representative data: not real client or production figures.

Marketplace Listings

What is happening?

Each brand sets a MAP (Minimum Advertised Price): the lowest price a marketplace is allowed to advertise a product at, usually with some allowed discount margin. This tool flags any listing whose selling price falls below that allowed floor (MAP minus a 10% allowance) as a potential violation, and shows exactly how far below.

Under the Hood
Brand selection ↓ Filter listings for that brand ↓ Compute allowed floor = MAP × 0.9 ↓ Flag listings selling below the floor

Inputs

  • MAP price, list price, selling price per marketplace

Processing

  • Allowed floor calculation
  • Discount % calculation
  • Threshold comparison

Output

  • Per-listing status
  • Violation amount

Technology

  • Vanilla JavaScript
  • Runs entirely in your browser
